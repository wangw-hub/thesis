# Terminology and Symbol Audit

Frozen terms: 非连续时间约束、规范区间表示 \(I^*\)、层次覆盖 \(C(P)\)、
策略摘要 `policyDigest`、链上授权状态、能力结构 CAP2、共享 Nonce、
多 Verifier 一致性、Fail-Closed。

The chapter distinguishes capability Nonce from transaction Nonce, validator
node keys from transaction and signing keys, and the infrastructure-validation
chain from the formal-authorization chain. No claim binds `policyDigest` to
\(C(P)\).
