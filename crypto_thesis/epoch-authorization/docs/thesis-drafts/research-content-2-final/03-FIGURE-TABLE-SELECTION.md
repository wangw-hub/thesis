# Figure and Table Selection

The body retains eight data figures and six data tables. This set covers design,
distribution, paired uncertainty, three experimental factors, stage composition
and chain stability without repeating every old exploratory plot.

Two Mermaid diagrams describe the contract state transition and CAP2 sequence.
They are mechanism diagrams whose source is the chapter text, not data charts.
CPU efficiency is omitted because the frozen CPU field is cumulative process
time, not utilization. Memory differences were small and are reported as a
limitation rather than promoted to a redundant main figure.
